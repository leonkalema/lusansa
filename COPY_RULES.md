# Lusansa copy rules

All website copy and content follows these rules. They apply to every page, card,
button, caption, meta description, guide article and WhatsApp template. They sit
on top of the voice rules in WEBSITE_GUIDE.md §2. Where the two conflict, this
file wins. Added 2026-07-15 at the owner's request.

## How to write

- Sentences average 10 to 20 words. One idea per sentence. An occasional longer
  sentence is fine.
- Active voice at least 90% of the time.
- Common, concrete words. Numbers, dates, names and measurable facts over
  abstraction.
- Punctuation: periods, commas, question marks, and a colon for a list. Nothing else.
- Mix short and medium sentences. Do not stack clauses.
- Connect ideas with plain words: and, but, so, then.
- Vary paragraph length. Ask a genuine question at most once per 300 words, and
  answer it at once.

## What to avoid

- No semicolons. No em dashes. Rewrite as two sentences or join with a comma or "and".
- No banned words or phrases. The full list is long. The ones we are most likely
  to slip on: however, moreover, furthermore, therefore, additionally, ultimately,
  essentially, significant, innovative, efficient, dynamic, ensure, foster,
  leverage, utilize, enhance, enable, elevate, delve, seamless, robust, holistic,
  optimize, vibrant, in addition, while, although, because (as subordinate
  conjunction), due to, in order to, even though, despite, also, as well as,
  navigating, landscape (metaphor), testament, realm, in conclusion, in summary.
- Minimize hedging modals: might, could, would, may, tends to.
- Minimize these nouns: insight, perspective, solution, approach.
- No sentences with more than one verb phrase where it can be split.
- No chains of prepositional phrases. No stacked dependent clauses.
- No list items starting with transition words. No ALL CAPS for emphasis.
  No numbered headings unless someone asks for an outline.
- No apologies, no hedging, no clichés, no metaphors about journeys, music or
  landscapes. State facts directly.

## Test before publishing

Read each sentence alone. Does it hold one idea in plain words? Does it survive
the banned list? Then it ships. This matches the weaver test: every sentence is
a knot someone will pull at.

## Separators in UI text

Data lines like "50×100 ft · Wakiso · 15 min from Gayaza town" use the middot (·)
as separator, never an em dash.
